This page is for someone who has never used Bitcoin. Read it before touching a plate. One plate cannot move the long-term fund. It needs any two plates plus the wallet map.

✟

Never type the words into a phone or a website.

✟

Never photograph the plates.

✟

Never put both plates into one device.

✟

Never buy a device from a marketplace or a link in a message.

1

Find two plates in two places.

Leave the third where it is. You only need two.

2

Find the wallet map.

If it asks for a passphrase, that paper is stored away from the plates. Decrypt it on a trusted computer, or with someone who can follow the map sheet. The map is not the words.

3

Buy two signing devices. Two different makers.

A Jade from Blockstream’s own shop. A BitBox from BitBox’s own shop. Not two of the same box.

4

One plate per device.

Plate A into one device. Plate B into the other. Never both plates in one device.

5

Load the map on both.

So both devices are looking at the same fund. The path is m/48'/0'/0'/2'. That line is not a secret.

6

Send a small test first.

If the test arrives, the fund can move. If the devices disagree, stop. Don’t force it.

Note

The home plate is a decoy. Its words alone open a small wallet. That is not the long-term fund. The fund still needs a second plate. Don’t mistake the home balance for the whole savings.

If the wallet is the spending wallet.

The steps above move the long-term fund. The spending wallet works differently. It has one plate and one passphrase paper. No map. No second plate.

1

Find the plate.

One plate, in its place.

2

Find the passphrase paper.

It lives apart from the plate, never on it. Without it, the plate opens an empty wallet.

3

Find the owner’s note.

It says which device restores this wallet. If there is no note, buy that kind of device from the maker’s own shop. Never a used device, never a marketplace device.

4

Restore the plate plus the passphrase on the device.

Words go into the device only. Never a phone, never a website.

5

Send a small test first.

If the test arrives, the spending wallet can move. If the address looks wrong, stop.

Do not mistake the spending balance for the whole savings. The long-term fund still needs two plates and the map, from the steps above.
