Before you begin optional

Five minutes with a pencil. Check the threats you actually care about. The guide answers each one. This method exists for people who checked theft and disaster.

→ 1

Check the files

Every file comes from its own site. Hash and signature are checked. If either fails, the file is deleted. Two tools must agree before the work continues.

→ 2

Two boots

The same computer, used twice. The first boot stays on the network and only watches. The second boot is Tails: network off, one seed, then a full shutdown.

→ 3

Make the keys

Casino dice make the randomness. Faces 1–4 are kept, 5 and 6 are rolled again. The computer shuts down between keys. Two programs must print the same 24 words.

→ 4

Two of three

The wallet needs any two of the three keys to spend. One key alone cannot move the long-term fund.

→ ↻

Rehearse on testnet optional

The entire flow with worthless coins before any plate exists. Same clicks as mainnet, mistakes cost nothing. The plates come after you’re bored of succeeding on testnet.

→ 5

Practice spend

A small amount comes in. The first seed signs, the computer shuts down, the second seed signs. Then the payment is broadcast. Savings don’t move until this works.

→ 6

Metal + map

The three keys are stamped into titanium and kept at three addresses. The wallet map travels with the record of those places, and the map is not another key.

→ 7

House rules

The limits of the method. A convenience is never allowed to break one of them.

Before you begin optional

Five minutes with a pencil. Check the threats you actually care about. The guide answers each one. This method exists for people who checked theft and disaster.

→ 1

Check the files

Every file comes from its own site. Hash and signature are checked. If either fails, the file is deleted. Two tools must agree before the work continues.

→ 2

Two boots

The same computer, used twice. The first boot stays on the network and only watches. The second boot is Tails: network off, one seed, then a full shutdown.

→ 3

Make one key

Casino dice make the randomness. Faces 1–4 are kept, 5 and 6 are rolled again. Two programs must print the same 24 words.

→ 4

Add the passphrase

A word or phrase added to the 24 words, written on paper. The paper lives apart from the plate, never on it, never in the same drawer.

→ 5

Stamp the plate

The 24 words are stamped into one titanium plate, kept apart from the device and apart from the passphrase paper.

→ 6

Watch-only plus test send

Sparrow watches the wallet without holding the seed. A small test send comes first, before real money moves.

→ 7

Recovery card

One card with the locations filled in by hand, kept apart from the plate and the passphrase paper.

→ 8

The limits of the method. A convenience is never allowed to break one of them.
