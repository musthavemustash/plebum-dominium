Easy: words into one trusted device

Trust: one device and its maker. Work: lowest.

  • One device, one maker. If the device fails, the firmware is flawed, or the shop sold a tampered device, the wallet fails with it.
  • One seed. Anyone who holds the 24 words holds the coins. There is no second key to stop a thief.
  • Words go into the device itself, never into a computer, phone, or website. If the words ever go elsewhere, treat that seed as exposed and start again with fresh words.
  • Stamp the words in titanium first, before the device entry. If the device fails during setup, the plate is the backup.
  • No passphrase on this path. A found plate alone can restore the wallet on a compatible device.
  • Keep the plate and the device in different places. Together, they are the wallet.

One key: one key plus a passphrase

Trust: one key, one device, one passphrase. Work: medium.

  • One key and one passphrase. Lose the passphrase and the wallet is gone, even with the plate and the device in hand.
  • Keep the passphrase on paper apart from the plate. If the plate and the passphrase paper are found together, the passphrase adds nothing.
  • One device, one maker, with the same device risks as Easy. A flawed or tampered device can still fail you.
  • Words and passphrase go into the device itself during recovery, nowhere else. No hardware company will ever ask for your seed phrase.
  • Check the receiving address on the device screen, not only on the computer screen, before you accept coins.
  • The watch-only wallet can watch, it cannot spend alone. Spending needs the device and the passphrase together.

Full program: three keys, three places

Trust: no single key, device, or place. Work: highest.

  • Most steps to get right. A missed step in any key can lock the wallet later.
  • Lose two keys and the map, and the funds are locked. Keep three plates in three places, and keep the encrypted map apart from any single plate.
  • Two seeds alone cannot rebuild the third key. Without the descriptor, two keys can spend but cannot rebuild the wallet from scratch. Keep the encrypted map safe.
  • No passphrase on the 2-of-3. The protection comes from three keys in three places, not from a passphrase.
  • Practice spend first, and rehearse on testnet if you have not done this before. Savings do not move until the practice spend works.
  • Shut down fully between keys. A key left in memory after a boot can leak into the next key.
