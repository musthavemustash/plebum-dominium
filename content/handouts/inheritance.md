# If I am gone

This page is for a spouse who has never used Bitcoin. It is read before a plate is touched. One plate cannot spend the long-term fund. The fund needs any two plates and the wallet map. The words are never typed into a phone.

## Do not

- Do not type the words into a phone.
- Do not type the words into a website.
- Do not photograph the plates.
- Do not put both plates into one device.
- Do not buy the devices from a marketplace listing or a link in a message.

## Do this

1. 01 · Find two plates in two places. Leave the third where it is.
2. 02 · The map is found. If it asks for a passphrase, that paper is stored away from the plates. The map is decrypted on a computer that is trusted, or with someone who can follow the map sheet. The map is not the words.
3. 03 · Buy a Jade from Blockstream’s own shop. Buy a BitBox from BitBox’s own shop. Two makers. Not two of the same box.
4. 04 · Put plate A into one device. Put plate B into the other. Never both plates in one device.
5. 05 · Load the map so the devices are looking at the same fund. The path is m/48'/0'/0'/2'. This line is not a secret.
6. 06 · Send a small test first. If the test arrives, the fund can move. If the devices disagree, stop and do not force it.

## The plate at home is a decoy

The words on the home plate, alone, open a small wallet. That is not the long-term fund. The long-term fund still needs a second plate. Do not treat the home balance as the whole savings.
