Stacked labels, three treatments

Same filling-file animation in all three. CREATE and BROADCAST TRANSACTION are now stacked two-line labels at identical heights, each with the file to its right. ONLINE sits top-center, OFFLINE bottom-center, and the red line stops short of the bottom label.
