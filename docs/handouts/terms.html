Terms

Ten words that carry the whole method. Everything else is built from these.

entropy
The randomness from the dice — the raw material the 24 words are built from.
airgap
Tails booted with networking Disabled. The computer forgets the session on shutdown.
2-of-3
Any two of three keys can spend. One alone cannot.
m/48'/0'/0'/2'
The wallet’s path. Confirmed on screen, never typed from memory. Not a secret.
PSBT
The unsigned payment file, carried on the USB. The seed never leaves the offline computer.
descriptor / xpub
The wallet map / a watch key. Both watch, neither spends.
BIP39
The 2,048-word list the 24 words come from.
hash · fingerprint
The file’s fingerprint must match the project’s published one — every character.
signature
The project confirming the file. “Good signature” — or the file is deleted.
zero-trust
Nobody’s word is taken. Two tools must agree.
