Checklist

Everything on the table before the first boot. Nothing is ordered mid-build.

On the tableThe dice table

  • Five casino dice with readable faces
  • Pencil and the recording grid
  • A private room — no camera

The computerOne machine, two boots

  • Boot 1: stays online, watch-only, never holds a seed
  • Boot 2: Tails USB, networking off
  • Data USB: carries only checked files

FilesChecked before anything goes offline

  • Tails 7.13 · Sparrow 2.5.5 (exact) · Electrum 4.8.2
  • Ian Coleman page (offline only) · dice converter (in this kit)

MetalThree plates, three places

  • Three titanium plates — one seed on each
  • StampSeed kit holds 1 (buy 2 more) · Cryptotag holds 2 (buy 1 more) — don’t mix methods
  • Three places, far enough apart that one disaster can’t take all three

Nothing is bought mid-build. If a plate or USB is missing when its sheet arrives, stop and get it first.
