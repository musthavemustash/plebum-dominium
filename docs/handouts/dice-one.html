Time30-45 min
DifficultyModerate
Steps4 sections

You will need: 5 dice · Paper and pen · The worksheet

You will roll dice to create one Bitcoin seed phrase. Each one becomes 24 words. The computer never sees the network while you work.

Safety first: Never enter seed words on an online computer. Boot Tails with networking Disabled before you start. Shut down fully between each key.

What you will do

  1. Boot Tails offline. Copy the dice converter page.
  2. Roll dice. Enter the faces. The page counts to 256 bits.
  3. Write down the 24 words. Prove them with a second tool.
  4. Repeat for Seed 2 and Seed 3, with a full shutdown between each.

Step 1 / 6

Boot Tails. Copy the converter.

Boot Tails with networking set to Disabled. Copy the checked dice converter page from the data USB to the Desktop.

Check: The page opens in Tor Browser and shows the offline indicator. If it says online, stop.

Step 2 / 6

Roll the dice. Enter the faces.

Roll five dice at once. Enter each face as you roll it. Faces 1 to 4 count as bits (1=00, 2=01, 3=10, 4=11). Faces 5 and 6 are skipped, roll those dice again. The page counts the pairs and stops you at 128.

Roll as many times as needed to get 256 bits of entropy: about 39 shakes.

Check: The page shows 128 pairs and 256 bits. About 39 shakes.

Step 3 / 6

Write the 24 words.

At 256 bits the page prints 24 words. Word 24 is the check. Do not invent it. Do not "fix" it. Write all 24 on the grid. Read them back out loud once.

Check: The last word is marked as the check word on the page.

Step 4 / 6

Prove it with a second tool.

Pick one: the Ian Coleman page takes the same rolls, no terminal. Or the Python converter takes the bits from the page, in the terminal. The 24 words must match exactly. If they differ, stop. Do not pick the prettier list.

Check: Both tools show the same 24 words.

Step 5 / 6

Shut down. Start the next key.

Shut Tails down fully. Wait until the screen is dark. Then boot again for Seed 2. New rolls, new words. Repeat for Seed 3. Never continue a list from the previous boot.

Check: Each seed has its own 24 words on its own grid. Three grids total.

Step 6 / 6

Confirm the checks agree.

The last word carries a short check so copying mistakes are caught. At least two checks must agree: the two tools showing the same words, and the wallet (Sparrow or Electrum, offline) opening the list without error. If anything disagrees, stop.

Stop if: The page says online. The two tools show different words. The wallet rejects the list. Any of these means stop and start that key over.
