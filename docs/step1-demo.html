Step 1 of 6

Boot Tails from the USB. The Welcome to Tails screen appears.

  1. 1. Boot Tails from the USB. The Welcome to Tails screen appears.
  2. 2. In the Additional Settings section at the bottom of the Welcome screen, tap the plus button in the gray bar below the text.
  3. 3. In the Additional Settings dialog, tap the Offline Mode row.
  4. 4. In the Offline Mode dialog, tap Disable all networking. Leave Enable networking (default) unselected.
  5. 5. Tap Add at the top right to add the setting.
  6. 6. Tap Start Tails at the top right of the Welcome screen. The desktop loads with no local network and no Tor Connection assistant.
