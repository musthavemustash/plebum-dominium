Plebum Dominium

(PLAY-boom doh-MIN-ee-oom)

Absolute Ownership by the Common People

“Pleb” is an everyday Bitcoiner. “Dominium” is Latin for absolute ownership. This site is for the Bitcoiner who wants to build a secure, self-custody Bitcoin wallet from the ground up, bypassing the need for a company, an app, or a custodian to manage or store access to their funds.

This guide shows you how to generate entropy with dice, import it into verified software, and stamp a 2-of-3 multisig wallet into titanium. It teaches you how to spend, view, and secure Bitcoin at various custody levels, with a hardware wallet completely optional.

Try it: tap a key, then tap a second key.

What this is

A guide to holding your own Bitcoin

Plebum Dominium is a step-by-step guide to Bitcoin self-custody at home. You generate keys with dice, verify every file you download, and stamp the words into titanium. No company holds your coins. No account can freeze them.

New to Bitcoin? Start here first, then come back.

Why this guide

Single point of failure

A single point of failure is any instance that can potentially compromise the wallet.

This guide teaches Bitcoiners how to create their own Bitcoin self-custody wallet, with optional hardware afterwards.

There are two setups. They differ in difficulty and in how much trust they ask you to place in any one device or maker. The full program is the most secure. It removes the most single points of failure.
