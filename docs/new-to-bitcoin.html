PLEBUM DOMINIUM

New to Bitcoin? Start here.

This guide teaches you to hold your own Bitcoin, with no company in the middle. Before you start, it helps to understand what Bitcoin is and what holding it yourself actually means. This page gives you that foundation.

What Bitcoin is

Bitcoin is digital money that no government or company controls. It runs on a network of computers around the world. There are only 21 million bitcoin that will ever exist. You can send it to anyone, anywhere, without asking permission from a bank.

When you own bitcoin, what you really own is a secret: a long random number called a private key. Whoever knows that secret can spend the coins. That is the whole game. Protect the secret and the coins are yours. Lose it and they are gone. There is no password reset.

What self-custody means

Most people buy bitcoin on an exchange, which holds the coins for them. That is convenient, but the exchange can freeze your account, get hacked, or go bankrupt. Self-custody means you hold the secret yourself. No company stands between you and your money.

This guide is about self-custody done carefully. You generate the secret with physical dice, write the recovery words on paper, stamp them into titanium, and store them in separate places. It takes work. The payoff is that no one can take your bitcoin except you.

What this guide does not do

This guide does not tell you to buy bitcoin, where to buy it, or when. It does not give financial advice. It teaches one skill: how to hold bitcoin yourself, securely, once you have some.

You should also know that self-custody is not for everyone. It puts the full responsibility on you. If that sounds like too much, there is no shame in keeping coins on a reputable exchange while you learn. Come back to this guide when you are ready.

Ready to start

When you understand the basics above, choose your path. The guide offers two ways to hold your own keys. Pick the one that matches the failure you worry about most.
